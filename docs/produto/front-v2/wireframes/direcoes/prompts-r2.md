# Prompts das imagens (rodada 2: minimalista)

Pedido do dev: muito menos explosivo, minimalista, a informação toda continua existindo mas aparece aos poucos. Referências escolhidas por ele: Linear/Raycast e Teenage Engineering. As 6 imagens têm o MESMO visual; o que muda entre elas é a organização da tela.

Para montar cada prompt: junte o bloco **FRAME**, o bloco da imagem e o bloco **VISUAL** (nessa ordem), literalmente. Paisagem 1536×1024, qualidade alta. Nome do arquivo no título do bloco.

---

## FRAME

A desktop app screen for Clipes, a free open-source app that turns long livestreams into vertical 9:16 Cortes with word-by-word captions. Windows app at 1440×900, rendered edge to edge with crisp readable Portuguese text, no browser window, no device, no desk. The screen is calm and sparse: only what the current task needs is visible, everything else is one click or key away.

## VISUAL

Visual law: minimal, in the spirit of Linear and Raycast with the rigor of Teenage Engineering. A near-black graphite ground; regions are separated by spacing and one subtle step of surface tone, almost no borders, no boxes around everything, no shadows, no gradients. One neutral sans at small sizes with tabular figures for timecodes; hierarchy comes from weight and two or three shades of grey text, not from colour. The only colour on the whole screen is a single signal orange, used sparingly: the playhead, the current selection mark and the one primary button. Status is shown with small icons and plain words, never with coloured badges. Small keyboard hints in muted grey. Never use the words "IA" or "AI" anywhere.
